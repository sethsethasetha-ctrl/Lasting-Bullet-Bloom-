Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lasting Bullet Bloom (the “Game”) handles information when you use the Game.

Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores gameplay settings and personal-best records locally on your device. These may include sound and vibration preferences, tutorial status, best survival time, best kill count, best level, and best hit value. This information is used only to operate the Game and is not sent by the Game to our servers.

Advertising

The Game may offer optional rewarded advertisements. To load, measure, and protect those advertisements, third-party advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising interaction data, diagnostics, and other information permitted by your device settings and applicable law.

Advertising partners can include Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies. Where required, the Game will request your permission before accessing the advertising identifier for tracking.

Configuration Requests

When the Game checks which rewarded advertisement placements are available, it sends the Game’s bundle identifier to the configuration service. This request is used only to retrieve advertising configuration.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising partner, please contact that partner directly and use the contact method below to notify us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information processed for advertising may be shared with the applicable advertising provider and its service providers as described in that provider’s privacy policy.

Your Choices

You can manage app tracking permission in iOS Settings. You can also limit ad tracking and manage privacy controls through your device settings. You may choose not to watch optional rewarded advertisements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Game, our advertising partners, or applicable law. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.

Contact

Before publishing this policy, replace this paragraph with your verified support email address or support URL. For privacy questions, users should be able to contact the developer through the support contact displayed on the Game’s App Store product page.
